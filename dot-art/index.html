Frequently Asked Questions

What is dot art?
Dot art uses Unicode Braille patterns (U+2800–U+28FF) and block characters to create pixel-style images made of dots. Each Braille character represents up to 8 dots in a 2×4 grid — giving fine resolution in pure text.
Where can I use dot art?
Dot art works in Discord messages, TikTok bios and comments, WhatsApp statuses, Instagram bios and captions, Twitter/X posts, Reddit posts, and most social media bios. Shorter pieces work best for name and bio fields.
Why does dot art look wrong on some phones?
Braille and block Unicode characters render differently across fonts. Most modern phones (iOS 14+, Android 10+) display them correctly. Heart and flower pieces tend to be the most consistent across all devices.
Does dot art work in Discord?
Yes — Discord fully supports Braille and block Unicode in messages and nicknames. Multi-row pieces look great in messages. Single-line pieces work best in nicknames.
Does dot art work on TikTok?
Yes. TikTok bios and comments fully support dot art. Multi-line pieces create striking pixel art banners in TikTok bios.
What characters are used in dot art?
Most pieces use Braille patterns (U+2800–U+28FF), block elements (█ ▀ ▄ ▌ ▐), and circle characters (●○). Braille gives the finest resolution — each character represents 8 dots in a 2×4 grid.
Can I use dot art in WhatsApp Status?
Yes. WhatsApp Status and messages support all Unicode including Braille and block characters. Copy any piece and paste directly — it works on both iOS and Android.
How is dot art different from text art?
Dot art uses Braille and block Unicode to build pixel-like images from dots. Text art uses keyboard characters to create shapes. Dot art tends to look more like pixel art; text art has more variety in line-drawn designs.
