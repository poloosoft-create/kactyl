Privacy Policy

arabikeyboard.com  ·  Last updated: May 2, 2026

Welcome to arabikeyboard.com ("we", "our", or "the site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our free online Arabic keyboard tool, how we use it, and your rights. By using arabikeyboard.com you agree to the practices described below.

1 Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The information collected falls into two categories:

  • Usage data: anonymous data collected automatically by our hosting provider and Google Analytics (if enabled), including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
  • Local storage data: the text you type and your word-frequency preferences are saved locally in your browser's localStorage — this data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

2 Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: to understand how visitors use the site (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: to serve relevant ads through Google AdSense. Google may use the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Functional cookies: to remember your preferences (language, dark/light mode, font size).

You can opt out of Google's use of the DART cookie by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party cookies via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

3 Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense is a third-party ad network operated by Google LLC. As a third-party vendor, Google uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to arabikeyboard.com and/or other sites on the internet. For more information about how Google collects and uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

4 How We Use Your Information

The anonymous data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Understand which features are most used and improve the tool accordingly
  • Monitor site performance and fix technical issues
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.

5 Third-Party Services

arabikeyboard.com integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense / Google LLC — advertising
  • Google Fonts — typography (Inter, Tajawal fonts)
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

6 Children's Privacy

arabikeyboard.com is a free typing tool accessible to all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of advertising cookies at any time
  • The right to clear your locally stored preferences by clearing your browser's localStorage

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.
