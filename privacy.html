Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours. No personal data collected, no advertising, no tracking.

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to kactyl.com (“we”, “our”, or “the site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our free multilingual keyboard and Unicode tools platform, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The information collected falls into two categories:

  • Usage data: anonymous data collected automatically by our hosting provider, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
  • Local storage data: your theme preference (light/dark), saved favorite styles, and saved kaomoji are stored locally in your browser’s localStorage — this data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

Kactyl uses your browser’s localStorage — not HTTP cookies — to remember your preferences. Specifically:

  • kactyl_theme — your light/dark mode preference
  • ft_favs — your saved Fancy Text Generator styles
  • km_favs — your saved kaomoji favorites

This data is stored entirely in your browser and is never transmitted to our servers. We currently use no advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

kactyl.com integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — loads the Inter typeface from Google’s CDN. Google may log your IP address when serving font files.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

The anonymous usage data our hosting provider collects is used exclusively to:

  • Monitor site performance and fix technical issues
  • Understand which features and pages are most used
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

kactyl.com is a free typing tool accessible to all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to clear your locally stored preferences by clearing your browser’s localStorage

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@kactyl.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of kactyl.com after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at hello@kactyl.com or visit our Contact page.
