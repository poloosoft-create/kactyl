Frequently Asked Questions

What is today's Hijri date?
Today's Hijri date is calculated automatically and shown at the top of this page in both Arabic and English. The calculation uses the standard Tabular Islamic Calendar (Kuwaiti algorithm), which is accurate to within 1 day of the observed date.
What is the difference between Hijri and Gregorian?
The Hijri (Islamic) calendar is a lunar calendar of 12 months totalling 354 or 355 days per year — about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian (solar) calendar. This is why Islamic events like Ramadan fall on different Gregorian dates each year, moving earlier by roughly 11 days annually.
How accurate is this Hijri calendar?
This calendar uses the algorithmic Hijri date, which is accurate to within 1-2 days. Actual Islamic months begin upon confirmed moon sighting (hilal), which is announced by religious authorities and may differ by one day from the calculated date depending on location.
What year is it in the Hijri calendar?
The current Hijri year is 1447 AH (Anno Hegirae). The Islamic year 1448 AH begins when the new moon of Muharram is sighted, expected around early July 2026. The Hijri calendar started in 622 CE, the year of the Prophet Muhammad's migration (Hijra) from Mecca to Medina.
How do I convert a Hijri date to Gregorian?
Use the Date Converter section above. Click the swap button (⇄) so the direction shows Hijri → Gregorian, enter the Hijri day, month, and year, then click Convert. The result shows the equivalent Gregorian date instantly.
When is Ramadan 2026?
Ramadan 1447 AH began in early March 2026. Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around late February 2027. The exact start date depends on the moon sighting in your country.
