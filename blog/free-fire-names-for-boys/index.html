How to Pick the Perfect Free Fire Name

  • Keep it under 12 characters if you want to add a border — most border templates add 4–8 characters.
  • Avoid spaces — Free Fire names with spaces can look broken in the kill feed on some devices.
  • Test readability — a name in Fraktur style looks cool in the menu but can be hard to read at small sizes. Bold or Double Struck styles stay clear at every size.
  • Check the character counter — the Kactyl generator shows your count in real time so you never go over 20.
