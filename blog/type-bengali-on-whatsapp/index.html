You can't type Bengali straight into WhatsApp unless your phone already has a Bengali keyboard installed — WhatsApp just borrows whatever keyboard your phone gives it. The fast way around that, without changing a single phone setting: type your message on kactyl.com/bengali/, copy it, and paste it into the chat.

Banglish vs বাংলা

Most casual Bengali chat is “Banglish” — Bengali written in English letters (“kemon acho” instead of কেমন আছো). Everyone reads it, but it loses the clarity and warmth of the real script. Kactyl types বাংলা for greetings, the poetry Bengalis love to share, and formal messages.

Juktakkhor — the Conjuncts That Render Oddly

Bengali fuses consonants into conjuncts (ক্ষ, ঞ্চ, ন্ত) that look like brand-new shapes. On older phones the fusion can fail and show the parts separately, or drop a hasant. Preview in the Kactyl editor; modern phones render the ligatures correctly.

Why Bengalis Type Phonetically (the Avro Habit)

Bengali’s most popular keyboard, Avro, lets you type phonetically in Latin and get Bengali out (“kemon” → কেমন). Kactyl’s Bengali keyboard works the same click-and-phonetic way, so the habit carries straight over — no new muscle memory.
