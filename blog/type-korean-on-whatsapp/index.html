You can't type Korean straight into WhatsApp unless your phone already has a Korean keyboard installed — WhatsApp just borrows whatever keyboard your phone gives it. How Hangul Builds Into Blocks as You Type

Korean isn’t typed letter-after-letter along a line — you tap a consonant, a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant, and they auto-assemble into a square block (ㅎ + ㅏ + ㄴ → 한). Kactyl’s keyboard composes the blocks the same way, so what you paste into WhatsApp is proper 한글, not loose jamo.

ㅋㅋㅋ, ㅠㅠ and Korean Chat Shorthand

Korean messaging runs on single-jamo shorthand: ㅋㅋㅋ (kkk) is laughing — the Korean “lol” — ㅠㅠ is crying, ㅇㅇ is “yeah / ok”. They’re lone Hangul letters sent on their own, and Kactyl’s keyboard has every one of them.

Romanization Won’t Cut It

Unlike some languages, Korean is almost never written in Latin between Koreans — 안녕 is never sent as “annyeong”. So a Hangul keyboard, or copy-paste from Kactyl, isn’t a nice-to-have; it’s the only natural way to message in Korean.
