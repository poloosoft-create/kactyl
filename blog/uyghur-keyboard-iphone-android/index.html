Uyghur Keyboard for iPhone & Android (Free, No App)

📅 Published 2026-06-02 ⏱ 4 min read 📱 Works on all phones
← Back to Blog 📱 Mobile guide 🔤 Uyghur Arabic script, right-to-left, 32 letters
Quick Answer The easiest way to type Uyghur on iPhone or Android is to use kactyl.com — open it in your phone browser, type your text, then copy and paste it into any app. No app download or system settings required.

The Problem with Typing Uyghur on Mobile

Most people who want to type Uyghur on their iPhone or Android run into the same problem: to use the built-in Uyghur keyboard, you need to go into Settings, add a new language, and switch keyboards every time you want to type in Uyghur. This switches your entire interface, can be confusing, and disrupts your normal usage.

There's a much simpler solution that requires none of that.

How to Type Uyghur on iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open Safari on your iPhone and go to kactyl.com/uyghur/
  2. Tap the Uyghur letters on the on-screen keyboard. The text appears in the editor above the keyboard.
  3. Tap Copy — your Uyghur text is now in your iPhone clipboard.
  4. Paste anywhere — open WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram, Notes, or any app, long-press and tap Paste.
iPhone Tip Bookmark kactyl.com/uyghur/ on your iPhone Home Screen (share button → "Add to Home Screen") so you can open the Uyghur keyboard with one tap, just like an app.

How to Type Uyghur on Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome on your Android phone and go to kactyl.com/uyghur/
  2. Tap the Uyghur letters on the keyboard. Your text builds up in the text editor.
  3. Tap Copy to copy your complete Uyghur text.
  4. Switch to any app — WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram — and long-press to paste.

Why the Copy-Paste Method is Better Than Installing a Language

Installing a Uyghur language keyboard on your phone changes your device settings, switches your interface language, and requires manual switching between keyboards. The Kactyl copy-paste method keeps your phone exactly as it is — you just have one browser tab open when you need to type Uyghur.

This is especially useful for:

  • People who type in Uyghur occasionally but not daily
  • Students or learners who need to type Uyghur for assignments
  • Diaspora users who communicate in both their native language and English
  • Anyone who needs to type Uyghur on a device they don't own

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download an app to type Uyghur on iPhone?+
No. Open kactyl.com/uyghur/ in Safari on your iPhone. No app download required — the browser is all you need. Works on iPhone 6 and newer.
How do I type Uyghur on an Android phone?+
Open Chrome on your Android and go to kactyl.com/uyghur/. Tap the Uyghur letters to type, then use the Copy button and paste into any app.
Can I save the keyboard to my phone home screen?+
Yes. On iPhone, use Safari's share button and choose 'Add to Home Screen'. On Android, use Chrome's menu and choose 'Add to Home Screen'. This creates a shortcut that opens the keyboard like an app.
Does the keyboard work without internet?+
The keyboard needs an internet connection to load initially. Once loaded, most typing features work even with a slow connection. Your typed text is auto-saved in the browser so you won't lose it.
