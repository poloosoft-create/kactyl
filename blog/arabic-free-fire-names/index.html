Why Arabic Names Stand Out in Free Fire

Most Free Fire lobbies are filled with Latin-script names. An Arabic name in the kill feed is visually distinct — it's immediately recognizable and memorable. Arabic calligraphic shapes are also naturally ornate, so even a plain name like خالد looks stylistic next to a generic "Shadow123".

Combined with a border template, an Arabic name becomes one of the most striking you can have in the game.

How to Use an Arabic Name in Free Fire

  1. Open the Kactyl FF Name Generator.
  2. Scroll down to the Arabic section at the bottom of the page.
  3. Type your name in Arabic using the on-screen keyboard or paste from the tables above.
  4. Pick a border template from the Borders section.
  5. Tap Copy FF Name to copy your styled Arabic name.
  6. Open Free Fire → Profile → tap your display name → paste.
Arabic Tip Arabic names look best with border templates that are symmetrical, like NAME or ★恁NAME恁★. Avoid left-only borders since Arabic reads right-to-left and the frame will feel unbalanced.

Arabic Names in PUBG Mobile

PUBG Mobile also supports Arabic script in player names. All the names in the tables above work in PUBG Mobile as well. The only difference is the character limit: PUBG allows 16 characters vs Free Fire's 20.

Arabic names tend to be shorter (3–6 letters) so they're actually better suited for PUBG's tighter limit. A name like سيف (Saif, 3 letters) with border NAME is only 5 characters total — perfect for PUBG with room for extra symbols.
