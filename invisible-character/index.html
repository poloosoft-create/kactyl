About Invisible Characters

Invisible characters are real Unicode characters that take up space (or no space) in text but render as completely blank to the human eye. They're not spaces — they're distinct Unicode codepoints with specific technical purposes, which is why they bypass filters that block regular spaces in usernames, bios, and message fields.

The most popular invisible character for gaming is the Hangul Filler (U+3164) — a full-width blank character from the Korean Hangul Unicode block that was originally used as a placeholder in Korean text processing. Games like PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, and Roblox allow Hangul Filler in usernames because it passes their character validation as a legitimate Unicode character, making your name appear completely blank to other players.

For messaging apps like WhatsApp and Telegram, a Non-Breaking Space (U+00A0) or Zero Width Space (U+200B) sends a "blank" message that doesn't trigger the empty-message check. For Instagram and TikTok bios, the Braille Pattern Blank (U+2800) creates invisible line spacing without the platform collapsing the empty lines. Each platform has quirks — which character works depends on how the platform validates input.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send a blank WhatsApp message?
Copy the Non-Breaking Space (U+00A0) or the Hangul Filler (U+3164) from this page, paste it into your WhatsApp message field, and send. WhatsApp checks that a message isn't "empty" using its own logic — these Unicode characters register as content, bypassing that check. The recipient sees a blank message.
Which invisible character works for PUBG and Free Fire names?
The Hangul Filler (U+3164) is the most reliable for PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, and similar mobile games. Copy it from the main button at the top of this page. Some games require multiple copies — use the generator to create 3–5 characters and paste them as your username. If one character type doesn't work, try the Braille Pattern Blank (U+2800) as an alternative.
Is using invisible characters allowed in games?
Most games don't explicitly ban invisible character usernames, since they pass standard character validation. However, game policies vary — some games may reset blank usernames in future updates. Using invisible characters in usernames is widespread and generally tolerated, but check your specific game's terms of service if you're concerned.
Why doesn't copy-paste work when I try to copy an invisible character manually?
Invisible characters can't be selected by eye — you can't see where they are to double-click them. Clicking any card on this page programmatically copies the exact Unicode character to your clipboard using the Clipboard API, ensuring you get the right character without accidentally copying surrounding content or missing the character entirely.
What is the difference between a space (U+0020) and an invisible character?
A regular space (U+0020 Space) is filtered out by most platforms' "field is empty" checks — you can't send a WhatsApp message with only spaces, and most games reject usernames with only spaces. Invisible Unicode characters like U+3164 and U+200B are different codepoints that pass these filters because the platform doesn't recognize them as "space." They look identical to spaces but behave differently technically.
