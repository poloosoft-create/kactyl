How Roblox Fonts Actually Work

Roblox lets you style your display name — the name above your avatar and in menus — up to 20 characters. The @username underneath is permanent and can't use styled text. Your display name can be changed (the first change is free, then it costs Robux), so it's worth getting right.

The thing that makes Roblox different from every other platform is its text filter. Roblox runs one of the strictest moderation systems online and it checks display names too — many Unicode symbols and unusual characters get blocked, tagged, or replaced with hashes, especially on accounts under 13. The styles that reliably pass are the clean alphabet ones: bold (𝗥𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗼𝘅), small-caps (ʀᴏʙʟᴏx) and serif or script letters — not heavy symbol combos.

Because of the 20-character cap, short names work best — some Unicode letters count as more than one, so watch the counter. Keep it to your name plus one clean style, and save the bracket-and-symbol combos (ꣁ ꣂ) for games that allow them, since Roblox's filter usually strips those out.

Styled names and Roblox's filter

Roblox is stricter than most platforms, because every name passes through its moderation filter. Your display name (3–20 characters) is where styled Unicode belongs — it appears above your avatar, on the leaderboard and in chat, while your underlying username stays the plain original you signed up with. Because the filter scans names, very unusual symbol combinations can be rejected or reverted, so lean toward clean, legible styles rather than dense decorative ones.

The safest, best-supported picks for a Roblox display name are small caps, bold and script — they read clearly at the small size Roblox shows names in and pass moderation without trouble. Avoid stacking combining marks or long symbol borders: they often get stripped, and they make your name hard to read in fast-moving chat. Set the styled version as your display name, keep it short so all of it fits, and test it in-experience to confirm it shows the way you expect.

The same rules apply to group and clan names: styled Unicode makes a tag recognisable on a leaderboard, but Roblox's filter still scans it, so a clean small-caps or bold tag passes far more reliably than a border of decorative symbols. If a name reverts after you save it, simplify the style rather than retrying the same characters — the filter is the reason, and a plainer style is the fix.

Everything you need to know about this generator

Why does my styled Roblox name get blocked or turned into hashes?
Roblox's text filter checks display names and rejects many Unicode symbols, especially on under-13 accounts. Stick to clean bold, small-caps or serif letters — those usually pass.
What's the Roblox display name character limit?
20 characters. Some Unicode letters count as more than one, so keep your styled name short — the counter above helps.
Can I change my Roblox @username to a fancy font?
No — the @username is permanent and standard-character only. Styling works on your display name, which you can change (first change free, then Robux).
Which font styles pass Roblox's filter?
Clean alphabet styles — bold, small-caps, serif, script. Heavy symbols and bracket combos usually get stripped or blocked.
Will other players see the same style?
Yes — display names are Unicode, so everyone sees the same style on PC and mobile.
Is it free?
The generator is free. Roblox itself only charges Robux if you change your display name after the first free change.
