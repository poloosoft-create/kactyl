Tigrinya Keyboard Online Free — Type Ethiopic Fidel From Any Device

Tigrinya is the official language of Ethiopia and Eritrea and the widely spoken Semitic language in the world after Arabic, with over 9 million native speakers. It is written in the Ge'ez script, known as Fidel (ፊደል) — a syllabic writing system where each symbol represents a consonant-vowel combination. The Fidel has 30 main consonant families, each with 7 vowel orders, giving over 210 unique characters. This free Tigrinya keyboard online gives you access to the complete Ethiopic Fidel from any device — no installation needed.

The keyboard uses a unique family-picker design: the 30 base consonants are displayed as a compact grid. Tap any consonant to reveal all 7 vowel forms in a popover — for example, tapping ሰ shows ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ. This is the most efficient way to type Tigrinya on a touchscreen. For desktop users, the Phonetic mode maps your QWERTY keys directly to Tigrinya characters: h→ሃ, l→ላ, m→ማ, s→ሳ, k→ካ, t→ታ, and more — so you can type Tigrinya as fast as you type English.
