About Crown Symbol

Crown symbols in Unicode primarily originated from the Chess Pieces block (U+2654–U+265F), where the queen ♛♕ and king ♚♔ pieces feature crown imagery. The six chess pieces — king, queen, rook, bishop, knight, and pawn — were included in Unicode 1.1 (1993), making chess the first game to receive dedicated Unicode encoding. This gives crown symbols a dual identity: they represent both royalty and the most powerful pieces in the world's most studied game.

The crown emoji 👑 (U+1F451), added in Unicode 6.0 (2010), dramatically expanded crown usage in digital communication. Unlike the text chess crowns, the emoji crown renders as a golden three-pointed crown and is used across social media to designate 'royalty' status — whether genuinely (monarchs, pageant winners) or playfully (self-proclaimed queens and kings in social posts). The trophy 🏆 and medal 🥇 frequently appear alongside crowns in recognition contexts.

In heraldry and formal symbolism, the Fleur-de-lis ⚜ (U+269C) and the Trident Emblem 🔱 (U+1F531) serve as traditional royal and noble symbols. The ⚜ appears on the Quebec flag and in Boy Scout uniforms worldwide. These heraldic symbols entered Unicode through the Miscellaneous Symbols block, which covers a wide range of cultural, religious, and ceremonial symbols from across human history.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the crown symbol called in Unicode?
The chess queen crowns are officially named: ♛ 'Black Chess Queen' (U+265B) and ♕ 'White Chess Queen' (U+2655). The king variants are ♚ 'Black Chess King' (U+265A) and ♔ 'White Chess King' (U+2654). The emoji 👑 is named 'Crown' (U+1F451). The Fleur-de-lis ⚜ is 'Fleur-De-Lis' (U+269C).
Can I use crown symbols in my Discord username?
Yes. ♛ ♕ ♚ 👑 all work in Discord usernames, nicknames, and status messages. The chess crowns ♛ ♕ are popular because they're text characters (always visible, no emoji rendering differences). The 👑 emoji may show slightly differently between desktop and mobile Discord apps.
What is the ⚜ symbol?
⚜ is the Fleur-de-lis (U+269C), a stylized lily used in heraldry since medieval France. It appears on the Quebec provincial flag, the New Orleans Saints NFL logo, and the Boy Scouts of America emblem. Its name means 'flower of the lily' in French. It's a popular decorative symbol in both formal and aesthetic social media content.
How do you use chess crown symbols in text?
Copy ♛ ♕ ♚ ♔ from this page and paste them anywhere. These chess symbols work in all text fields — they're plain Unicode characters with broad support. In a username like ♛ KingRoyal ♛, they create a distinctive visual frame. For formal chess notation, algebraic notation uses K and Q letters rather than the Unicode symbols.
