Gujarati Keyboard Online Free — Type Gujarati From Any Device

Gujarati (ગુજરાતી) is spoken by over 17 million native speakers and understood by 40+ million people across countries and communities worldwide. Written in the beautiful Devanagari script, Gujarati is the official language of Nepal and is one of the 22 scheduled languages of India. Millions of Nepalis use WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube daily — but typing Gujarati on a standard keyboard is difficult without the right tool. This free Gujarati keyboard online solves that problem instantly, on any device, with zero installation.

The Roman mode (also called Romanized Gujarati or transliteration mode) is the most popular feature on this page. It lets you type Gujarati words using the English QWERTY keyboard you already know. Type namaste → नमस्ते, dhanyabad → धन्यवाद, maya → माया, ramro → राम्रो — all automatically converted when you press Space. No need to memorize the Devanagari keyboard layout.
